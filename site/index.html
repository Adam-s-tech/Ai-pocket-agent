What's in the pocket

Chat, agents and research - on your terms.

Run a model on the phone itself, bring your own keys for the cloud, or reach the models on your own computer. Every conversation stays in the app's private storage.

On-device inference

llama.cpp on the phone, with Metal on iPhone and iPad. A curated catalog of GGUF models sized for phones - Qwen3, Llama 3.2, Gemma 3, Phi-4 mini, SmolLM2 - with a fit rating for your RAM, resumable downloads, Hugging Face search and GGUF import. Every llama.cpp sampler and cache control is there when you want it.

Cloud with your keys

Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek and many more, inference platforms like OpenRouter and Groq, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Agents with a plan

Five built-in agents plus your own. They keep a live plan checklist, use tools and write deliverables to a workspace you can open and share.

Deep research

Plans sub-questions, reads sources in parallel, reflects on gaps, then streams a report with inline citations and a source list.

Terminal

SSH to your servers with a key bar for Esc, Tab, Ctrl and arrows. Android and desktops also get a local shell. Agents can run commands - after asking.

Tools, MCP and skills

Web search, page reading, a JavaScript sandbox, memory and more, with per-tool Allow once / Always / Deny. Add MCP servers and SKILL.md skills.

Made for touch

Voice input, photos and documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and more) extracted on the device, streaming Markdown with visible thinking, swipe and long-press gestures, haptics, a notification when a background reply finishes - in 25 languages, dark or light, with six accent colors.

A drawer on phones, an inspector on iPads.

The layout adapts: a drawer on phones, a persistent sidebar on tablets, and sidebar plus inspector - plan, workspace files, sources - on wide iPads.

Stays in your pocket

Your data doesn't leave unless you send it.

  • Local storage. Conversations, files and memory live in the app's private storage.
  • Keys in the vault. API keys are kept in the Keychain or the Android Keystore.
  • Only where you point it. Messages go only to the provider you pick - or nowhere, with an on-device model.
  • You approve the tools. Allow once, always allow or deny, per tool.

reflect  gaps found → follow-up round
write    report with inline citations [1][2]
